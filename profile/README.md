## JsonEngine

Managed web scraping, price monitoring and ready-to-use e-commerce datasets.

We collect product, price and stock data from online stores and deliver it as clean, validated datasets: matched across retailers, with the store, ZIP and currency recorded next to every price.

**What you'll find here**

- [`shopify-products-json`](https://github.com/JsonEngine/shopify-products-json): a polite collector for Shopify's public `/products.json` feed, plus our test of 68 brand stores (page limits, the 25,000-product cap, short pages that break naive loops).
